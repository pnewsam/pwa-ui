# Code of Conduct

PWA UI is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, respect different experiences and identities, accept feedback in good faith, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations. They may issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the private contact method published with the source repository. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are involved in a report.
